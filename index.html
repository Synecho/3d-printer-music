What's in the recording?
Fine-tune detection

Too many wrong notes: lower the detection threshold (0.10) or raise the shortest note. Quiet notes missing: lower the volume threshold (−50). Octave jumps: narrow the pitch range. BPM only sets the bar grid.

Detection follows a single melodic line. Vocals, whistling or a single isolated instrument work well. With a full band mix it jumps between instruments, so isolate one track first, e.g. with a stem separator.

What the motors play
Range Bar to

Click the view to set the position. Drag to scrub: during playback it jumps along, when stopped you hear the note under the pointer. The dark flags at the top mark the start and end of the range and can be dragged. Keys: Space = play/pause, ← → = one bar, Home = back to start. The end bar is exclusive: start 5, end 9 = bars 5 to 8.

Advanced settings

Full steps/mm = steps per revolution ÷ (teeth × pitch). From Marlin: M92 value ÷ microsteps. From Klipper: 200 ÷ rotation_distance (400 ÷ rotation_distance for 0.9° motors).
Tuning offset: how many semitones was the test tone off? Too low = negative. One octave too low = −12.

How to start it
    Printer sounds off?

    1. Print the test tone G-code: 2 s A4 (440 Hz), a pause, 2 s A5 (880 Hz). Measure it with a tuner app on your phone.
    2. Enter the deviation as tuning offset under "Advanced settings".
    3. High notes sound off or are missing: turn off quiet mode on the printer or transpose down.
    4. Notes "whine" at the start: short notes don't quite reach their speed. Lowering the tempo a little helps.
